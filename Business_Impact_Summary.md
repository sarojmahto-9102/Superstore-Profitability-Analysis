# Business Impact Summary

## Key Findings

- Total Sales: $2,297,200.86
- Total Profit: $286,397.02
- Overall Profit Margin: 12.47%
- Technology generated the highest category profit: $145,454.95.
- Discounts above 40% resulted in a loss of $99,558.59.
- Discounts between 21% and 40% resulted in a loss of $35,817.47.
- Tables had the highest sub-category loss: $17,725.48.
- The Central region had the lowest profit margin: 7.92%.

## Recommendations

1. Review high-discount orders and introduce discount limits.
2. Investigate losses in Tables and Bookcases.
3. Analyze the Central region's low profit margin.
4. Focus on profitable products and categories.

## Expected Business Impact

These actions could help management reduce losses and improve profitability. The actual impact should be measured after implementation.
