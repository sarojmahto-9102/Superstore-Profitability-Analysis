Superstore Profit Analysis: Problem Statement

Business question
Where is Superstore losing profit, and is discounting the cause?

Why it matters
Superstore earns 2.30M in sales but keeps only a 12.5% profit margin. Management needs to know which products, regions and discount levels are reducing profit, so it can fix them.

Sub-questions
1. Which discount levels make orders unprofitable?
2. Which sub-categories and products lose the most money?
3. Which regions and segments have the weakest profit margin?

Data
Superstore sales data from 2014 to 2017 with 9,994 order lines (sales, quantity, discount, profit, region, category and customer segment).

Expected outcome
A clear analysis of the causes of loss, and practical recommendations for management, such as a discount policy.
